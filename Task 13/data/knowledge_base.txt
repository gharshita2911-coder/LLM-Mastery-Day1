SECTION 1: REFUND & RETURNS POLICY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 Overview
------------
Acme Corp is committed to customer satisfaction. If you are not fully satisfied
with your purchase, we offer a straightforward refund and returns process
designed to be simple, fair, and transparent.

1.2 Eligibility for Refunds
----------------------------
To be eligible for a refund, the following conditions must be met:

- The refund request must be submitted within 30 days of the original purchase date.
- The product must be unused and returned in its original packaging.
- Digital products and downloadable software are non-refundable once the
  license key has been activated.
- Annual subscription plans are eligible for a pro-rated refund if cancelled
  within the first 60 days.
- Monthly subscription plans can be cancelled anytime; refunds are issued only
  for the unused portion of the current billing cycle.

1.3 How to Request a Refund
-----------------------------
Step 1: Log in to your Acme Corp account at app.acmecorp.com.
Step 2: Navigate to Settings → Billing → Refund Request.
Step 3: Select the order or subscription you wish to refund.
Step 4: Choose a reason from the dropdown and provide a brief description.
Step 5: Submit the request. You will receive a confirmation email within 1 hour.

Alternatively, contact our billing team directly at billing@acmecorp.com with
your Order ID and registered email address.

1.4 Refund Processing Time
----------------------------
- Refund requests are reviewed within 2 business days.
- Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
- The refund is credited back to the original payment method (credit card,
  PayPal, or bank transfer).
- International refunds may take up to 10–14 business days depending on
  your bank or payment provider.
- You will receive an email notification once the refund has been processed.

1.5 Non-Refundable Items
-------------------------
The following are strictly non-refundable:
- Setup fees and onboarding fees.
- Customisation and professional services charges.
- Any product purchased under a promotional or discounted plan (unless defective).
- Add-ons or integrations purchased separately.

1.6 Disputed Charges
---------------------
If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, raise a dispute within 14 days
by emailing billing@acmecorp.com with the subject line "Disputed Charge – [Order ID]".
Our finance team will investigate and respond within 3 business days.

SECTION 2: ONBOARDING PROCESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 Overview
------------
The Acme Corp onboarding process is designed to get new customers and team
members productive as quickly as possible. The standard onboarding timeline
is 7 days for individuals and 14 days for enterprise teams.

2.2 Step-by-Step Onboarding for New Users
------------------------------------------
Day 0 — Account Creation
  - Sign up at app.acmecorp.com/register using your business email.
  - Verify your email address via the confirmation link sent within 5 minutes.
  - Set a strong password (min 12 characters, one uppercase, one number, one symbol).
  - Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Day 1 — Profile Setup
  - Complete your user profile: name, role, department, timezone.
  - Upload a profile photo (optional but recommended for team collaboration).
  - Connect your calendar (Google Calendar or Outlook) for meeting scheduling.
  - Set your notification preferences (email, Slack, in-app).

Day 2 — Product Walkthrough
  - A Welcome Kit email is sent with links to video tutorials and the Quick
    Start Guide.
  - An automated product tour launches on first login — takes approximately
    15 minutes to complete.
  - Explore the Dashboard, Projects, Reports, and Settings tabs.

Day 3 — Onboarding Call
  - A dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) will reach out to schedule a
    30-minute onboarding call.
  - The call covers: account configuration, team setup, integrations, and
    answering any initial questions.
  - Enterprise customers receive an additional 60-minute deep-dive session.

Day 4–5 — Integration & Configuration
  - Connect your existing tools: Slack, Jira, GitHub, Salesforce, or Zapier.
  - Import existing data using the CSV Import Tool under Settings → Import.
  - Configure user roles and permissions for your team members.
  - Set up your first project or workflow using the provided templates.

Day 6–7 — Go-Live & Handoff
  - Your CSM confirms the setup is complete and signs off on the onboarding
    checklist.
  - Access to the Acme Corp Help Center (help.acmecorp.com) is activated.
  - You are added to the monthly customer newsletter and product update emails.
  - A 30-day post-onboarding check-in call is automatically scheduled.

2.3 Enterprise Onboarding
--------------------------
Enterprise customers (50+ seats) receive an enhanced onboarding experience:
- A dedicated Implementation Manager assigned for the full onboarding period.
- Custom data migration support for existing databases or CRM systems.
- SSO (Single Sign-On) configuration with your identity provider (Okta, Azure AD).
- Custom role and permission mapping to match your organisational hierarchy.
- SLA-backed onboarding completion guarantee within 21 business days.

2.4 Self-Service Onboarding Resources
---------------------------------------
If you prefer to onboard at your own pace:
- Quick Start Guide: help.acmecorp.com/quickstart
- Video Tutorial Library: help.acmecorp.com/videos
- API Documentation: developers.acmecorp.com
- Community Forum: community.acmecorp.com
- Webinars (live and recorded): acmecorp.com/webinars

SECTION 3: SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 Web Application (Browser-Based)
-------------------------------------
The Acme Corp web application runs in any modern browser. No installation is required.

Supported Browsers:
  - Google Chrome 110 or later (recommended)
  - Mozilla Firefox 109 or later
  - Microsoft Edge 110 or later
  - Safari 16 or later (macOS and iOS)
  - Brave Browser (latest stable)

Minimum Hardware for Web App:
  - Processor: Dual-core 2.0 GHz or higher
  - RAM: 4 GB minimum, 8 GB recommended
  - Internet: Stable broadband connection, minimum 10 Mbps download speed
  - Screen Resolution: 1280 × 720 or higher

3.2 Desktop Application
-------------------------
The Acme Corp Desktop App is available for Windows and macOS.

Windows:
  - OS: Windows 10 (version 21H2) or Windows 11
  - Processor: Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or equivalent
  - RAM: 8 GB minimum, 16 GB recommended
  - Storage: 2 GB free disk space for installation
  - .NET Framework 4.8 or later required

macOS:
  - OS: macOS 12 Monterey or later
  - Processor: Apple M1 / Intel Core i5 or higher
  - RAM: 8 GB minimum, 16 GB recommended
  - Storage: 2 GB free disk space for installation
  - Rosetta 2 required for M1/M2 Macs running Intel builds

Linux (Beta):
  - Supported distros: Ubuntu 20.04 LTS, Ubuntu 22.04 LTS, Debian 11
  - Processor: x86_64 architecture required
  - RAM: 8 GB minimum
  - Storage: 2 GB free disk space

3.3 Mobile Application
-----------------------
  - iOS: iPhone or iPad running iOS 15 or later
  - Android: Android 10 or later, minimum 3 GB RAM

3.4 Self-Hosted / On-Premise Deployment
-----------------------------------------
For enterprise customers who require on-premise deployment:

Server Requirements:
  - OS: Ubuntu 20.04 LTS / 22.04 LTS or RHEL 8+
  - CPU: 8-core processor minimum (16-core recommended for 100+ users)
  - RAM: 32 GB minimum, 64 GB recommended
  - Storage: 500 GB SSD (NVMe preferred); scalable with additional volumes
  - Database: PostgreSQL 14+ or MySQL 8.0+
  - Cache: Redis 7.0+
  - Reverse Proxy: Nginx 1.20+ or Apache 2.4+
  - Docker: Docker 24+ and Docker Compose 2.0+ (for containerised deployment)

Network:
  - HTTPS on port 443 (TLS 1.2 or 1.3 required)
  - Outbound access to api.acmecorp.com on port 443 for license validation
  - Internal network bandwidth: minimum 1 Gbps between app and database servers

3.5 API Integration Requirements
----------------------------------
  - REST API: Any HTTP client supporting TLS 1.2+
  - Python SDK: Python 3.9 or later
  - Node.js SDK: Node.js 18 LTS or later
  - API Rate Limit: 1,000 requests/minute on Standard plan; 10,000 on Enterprise
  - Authentication: OAuth 2.0 Bearer Token or API Key

------------------

------------------------------

    Data is retained for 60 days after cancellation before permanent deletion.

SECTION 6: SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6.1 Data Security
------------------
  - All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3.
  - Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
  - Regular third-party penetration testing conducted quarterly.
  - SOC 2 Type II certified.
  - ISO 27001 certified.
  - GDPR compliant for EU customers.
  - Data residency options available for Enterprise customers (US, EU, IN).

6.2 Uptime & SLA
-----------------
  - Standard SLA: 99.9% uptime (approx. 8.7 hours downtime/year).
  - Professional SLA: 99.95% uptime.
  - Enterprise SLA: 99.99% uptime (approx. 52 minutes downtime/year).
  - Check live status at status.acmecorp.com.
  - Scheduled maintenance windows: Sundays 2:00 AM – 4:00 AM IST.
  - Customers are notified 72 hours in advance of planned maintenance.

6.3 Data Backup
----------------
  - Automated daily backups retained for 30 days (Standard).
  - Automated hourly backups retained for 90 days (Enterprise).
  - Point-in-time recovery available for Enterprise customers.
  - Backup storage is geographically distributed across 3 regions.
