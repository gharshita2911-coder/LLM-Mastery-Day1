TechNova Inc. – Employee Handbook (2025 Edition)

LEAVE POLICY
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance using the HR portal. Unused leave of up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next year; the remaining balance is forfeited. Sick leave is capped at 10 days per year and requires a medical certificate for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.

WORK FROM HOME
TechNova operates a hybrid work model. Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week. Work-from-home days must be agreed with the direct manager at least 24 hours in advance. Employees on probation (first 6 months) are not eligible for work-from-home arrangements.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year: in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, manager rating, and a 360-degree peer review. Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5. Employees scoring 4 or above for two consecutive reviews are eligible for the fast-track promotion programme.

TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT
Domestic business travel is reimbursed at actuals for flights and trains (economy class). Hotel accommodation is capped at Rs. 5,000 per night. Meals are reimbursed up to Rs. 600 per day. All claims must be submitted within 15 working days of returning from travel with original receipts.

RESIGNATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
Employees at junior levels (Grade 1–3) must serve a notice period of 30 days. Senior employees (Grade 4 and above) must serve 60 days. The company may choose to pay in lieu of notice. Garden leave may be granted at management discretion during the notice period.

ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY
TechNova maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards workplace harassment of any kind, including sexual harassment, bullying, and discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, or disability. All complaints must be reported to the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) within 3 months of the incident. Investigations will be completed within 90 days.

CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to maintain confidentiality of all business information. Use of company devices for personal social media during work hours is discouraged. Any conflict of interest must be disclosed to HR in writing within 7 days of becoming aware.
